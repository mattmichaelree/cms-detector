    Custom Search of Lobby Database

    Lobby registrations have been collected since 1998 and the information is searchable. However, it has only been since 2000 that other information such as activities reports could be submitted electronically. Reports filed before that time are not searchable.

    Searches work best with Chrome or Firefox browser. Your browser must support popups.

    Lobby Expenditure Lists

    Lobby expenditures go back to year 1993. Years prior to 2000 were partial totals and were not verified. Some final reports prior to 2017 were not included in the totals. Mass Media expeditures are not included in the Government Recipient list as they are not attributable to any governmental entity. Reports are updated quarterly.
